# Mode criteria

## Direction first

Every mode starts a new or changed direction as pitches, as the story criteria's **Direction pitch** says, and develops one only after the user approves it. An audit's findings stand on their own; a rewrite it proposes is put as a pitch first.

## Create

A complete new arc has original characters, a dramatic premise, the player's role, a sequence of meaningful developments, a final confrontation or decision, and an aftermath, and sits inside a frame that keeps the story open for the arcs after it, as the story criteria's **Room to continue** says. Existing mechanics constrain how it becomes playable. Unknown audience or production budgets are stated assumptions.

## Audit

The full implemented journey is the baseline, including first launch and onboarding. Read the canon’s appearances, learned concepts, relationships, unresolved setups, fulfilled payoffs, and preserved victories, then compare them with current implementation. Findings separate observed defects, inferred player-experience risks, and taste preferences. Each finding names evidence, player impact, a proposed repair, and affected consumers. Keeping a strong beat is a valid decision; preserving a weak beat because it already exists is not. A rewritten ending must still answer the original arc's questions, unless an explicit, justified premise change replaces them consistently. A story whose ending closes the game to new content, or that has no frame outliving its arcs, is audited as a structural defect and gets one, with any retcon it takes marked.

## Extend

The complete earlier journey, from onboarding through revisits, is input canon, including fulfilled promises, irreversible choices, relationships, and the ending players already experienced. New scope follows from that aftermath. A coda or changed final invitation may connect the arcs while preserving earned resolution. Retcons are explicit proposed changes, never silently revised history. New worlds have their own complete arcs, connective characters/themes, accessible revisits, and a final state even when later adventures remain possible.
