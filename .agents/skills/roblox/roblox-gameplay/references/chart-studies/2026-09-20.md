# Desktop US chart sample — September 20, 2026

Passive research of the first five visible **Top Playing Now** entries on the [Computer / United States chart](https://www.roblox.com/charts?device=computer&country=us), observed September 20, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). The full category retained the same order but dropped query filters; selection came from the original filtered page. This dated sample is not an evergreen ranking.

Each official experience page's description and leading promotional image were inspected. Brookhaven also exposed a muted official video: sampled frames showed school and café social scenes. No accessible playable video was found for the other four in the inspected state; additional carousel exploration timed out. No game was played. Sound quality, reward cadence, retention, neurological effects, and the causes of popularity were not measured.

Across this sample, understandable purposes, useful progression, variety, expressive identity, exploration, and social contribution recur in advertised promises. These are design hypotheses to test in our own player journeys, not proven causes of chart position. Frequent layered celebration remains the user's explicit design preference; this sample does not show that all five games use fast coin bursts or similar effects.

Apply these observations only where they fit the active game and authorized scope; this sample alone does not justify adding pets, currencies, trading, competitive theft, or new progression.
