# Shooters and Arena Games

The `roblox-game-overview` skill's pages for a match-based combat game, such as a team capture game
on rotating maps with a weapon loadout.

## Pages

- **Modes.** A `tree` of each mode with its dialog: the goal, team sizes, score or time limit, the
  respawn rule, and a `diagram` of a match from lobby to results.
- **Maps.** A `tree` of each map with its picture staged from a spawn or objective, the modes it
  runs and its objective points.
- **Loadout.** A `table` of each weapon or ability with damage, rate, range, ammo or cooldown and
  how it is unlocked; a `figures` section of the loadout screen.
- **Progression.** Levels, unlock tracks and currencies, on How to play.

## Consistency

- Every mode names maps that exist and every map has the spawns and objectives its modes look up.
- Each weapon's stated stats match the values it fires with.
- Unlock requirements name levels and items that exist, in an order a player can reach.
