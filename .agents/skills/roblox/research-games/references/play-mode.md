# Play mode

The `research-games` skill's procedure for each approved play turn: the runner, sign-in, and what a short session records.

Use the isolated runner for launches and play; when none exists or it cannot control the published client, provision or repair it as `roblox-studio`'s testing reference says. Non-interfering input alone is insufficient if launching clients steals focus or covers the user's work. Only when that route leaves a concrete blocker, return passive evidence with play mode blocked; do not seize host input or assume Studio MCP controls a published game.

Record the first success, time to understand the next action, meaningful decisions, felt input/feedback quality, and a few concrete moments of delight or frustration. Stop when onboarding ends or at the cap even if the loop encourages continued play. Avoid claiming long-term depth or retention from this short sample.
