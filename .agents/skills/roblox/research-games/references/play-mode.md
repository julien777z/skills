# Play mode

The `research-games` skill's procedure for each approved play turn: the runner and what a short session records.

Play in the isolated runner as `play-roblox-protocol` says; when none exists or it cannot control the published client, provision or repair it as `roblox-studio`'s testing reference says. A concrete blocker that route leaves returns passive evidence with play mode blocked, as **Reuse the isolated research environment** says.

Record the first success, time to understand the next action, meaningful decisions, felt input/feedback quality, and a few concrete moments of delight or frustration. Stop when onboarding ends or at the cap even if the loop encourages continued play. Avoid claiming long-term depth or retention from this short sample.
