# World-space text inspection

Use this reference for authored signs and messages rendered by SurfaceGuis,
BillboardGuis, decals, or textures, regardless of which system creates them.

Before changing a message, inspect its adornee and all text-bearing descendants,
including authored artwork and runtime writers. Give each message one presentation
owner. Repeated updates and rejoin must replace obsolete content rather than layer
new labels, textures, or GUIs over it. Preserve intentional artwork and separate
simultaneous messages into readable regions.

Choose surface size and orientation, canvas resolution or PixelsPerStud, font size,
padding, line spacing, contrast, and wrapping for the intended reading distance.
Shorten copy or enlarge the presentation when it cannot fit; TextScaled and valid
bounds do not establish readability. Inspect the full rendered message from normal
player height and each expected approach angle, on supported desktop and touch
viewports. Include the longest real content, relevant lighting, oblique views,
and scenery or character occlusion. An isolated close-up is insufficient.

Inspect initial and changed states after progression, repeated updates, and rejoin.
Record the surface, state, camera distance, and viewport. Confirm old text is gone
and every current word can be read without zooming or relying on source text. An
uninspected or illegible surface remains unverified or failed.
