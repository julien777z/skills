# Review rounds

A change gets the review rounds `acceptance-gate`'s **Bounds** allows, in every review loop. A
change that has used its rounds is not repaired again: findings that keep coming on one change say
the approach is wrong, not that the next patch will land. The stop exists to end wasted rounds,
never to add questions, and the task stays active.

## Counting

- A change, its rounds and each flag's own repair rounds are counted as `acceptance-gate`'s
  **Bounds** sets. Any change counts, whether source, tests, tooling, configuration, guidance or
  interface, and a skill test's round counts like any other check's.
- `review-proposal`'s attack rounds on a proposal keep their own count.

## Disposition At The Limit

- Put the disposition to `council` in **Ask** mode as **Bounds** says, with the change, each
  round's findings and remedies, and the different approach your reconsideration found. Record its
  ruling and follow it.
- If the authorized outcome still needs work, update the active plan and start the replacement, a
  new change with its own rounds, at its first step **Priority** permits, under the existing
  authorization and without a new plan. A different implementation idea is not a new
  approval boundary.
- Ask only when the replacement needs an unresolved product, scope, security, disclosure or
  authorization decision, and join that question to the questions already open rather than sending
  it separately.
- Report the decision in plain words, with current captures where the change is visible.
